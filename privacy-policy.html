Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Product / 产品: Privacy Cleaner Pro (Chrome Extension)

Last updated / 最后更新: 2026-04-10

English

Privacy Cleaner Pro is designed to help users clean webpage content locally in the browser. We respect your privacy.

1) Data Collection

Privacy Cleaner Pro does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal data. The extension does not require account registration and does not use remote servers to process your content.

2) Local Processing

All processing is performed locally on your device within the browser environment. We do not upload your browsing content to external servers.

3) Permissions

The extension only requests the minimum permissions required for its core functionality. Permissions are used solely to provide cleaning features requested by the user.

4) Third Parties

Privacy Cleaner Pro does not share data with third parties and does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

5) Children

This extension is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6) Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

7) Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: tianlan9308@gmail.com
